About

ESPBlaster / M5Gotchi is a port of Pwnagotchi to ESP32-S3 based M5Stack devices. It captures WPA/WPA2 handshakes, performs PMKID attacks, runs evil portals, and connects to the PWNGrid peer-to-peer mesh network — all from a pocket-sized device with a keyboard or button-driven UI.

Manual Flash

01 Put device in download mode — hold the BOOT button, press and release RST, then release BOOT.
02 Connect the device via USB and select it in the browser popup.
03 Click Flash — the merged firmware will be written at 0x0000.
04 Power cycle the device after flashing completes.
